# Find the Masked Man: Geoint and osint challenge

## What I know
 
- The picture was taken on December 3rd, 2023, around 18:00
- The location is in **Paris**, in a **central and upscale area**
- The scene is near the intersection of **a rue and an avenue**
## First moves
 
The first thing I did was adjust the saturation and clarity of the image, hoping to make out the lettering in the background.
 
I could read the ending "sserie Julien" and guessed it was *pâtisserie Julien*. Knowing French helped.
 
<img width="270" height="300" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9c61b66b-c729-400b-8015-aefd9639c8a8" />

## What to look for
 
From the image, these are the details I looked for while searching:
 
- A six-story building with a business on the ground floor
- A pâtisserie with "Julien" in its name, probably nearby
- An advertising panel on the pavement
- Possibly a bike dock close by
- Bike signs and a crosswalk painted on the road
<img width="480" height="597" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/efa3ce55-4c33-47a0-a4d7-cf39636269c6" />

 
## Searching
 
I searched "pâtisserie Julien" in Google Maps. One of the first results sat on the corner of a rue and an avenue, which matched what I already knew: Maison Julien, at 73 Avenue Franklin Delano Roosevelt, 75008 Paris.
