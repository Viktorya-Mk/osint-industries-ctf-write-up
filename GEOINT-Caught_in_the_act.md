# Caught in the Act: Geoint and osint Challenge

**Goal:** find the name of the university and the city.\
**Starting point:** a single photo.\
**Type:** GEOINT and OSINT.

## First impressions

What caught my attention first was the words "Library" and "Business", and the logo on the truck.\
<img height="500" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7681abb7-81a1-4fcf-95c4-ac0768803ee9" />

## Dead ends

I started by analyzing the logo, to see if I could find information on the company that could hint at which country this was in, or any link to working with universities. The quality was too low, so nothing came out of it.

Next I tried some reverse image searches, but I was unsuccessful.

## Changing approach: the building material

So I decided to focus on the building's material. I looked through the different types of stone that buildings are made of, and went toward sandstone and limestone, as they were closest in color.\
<img width="438" height="299" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0248627b-4cfb-4359-b7fb-31ee30597e54" />

After comparing what both materials look like on actual buildings, I concluded it was sandstone, and found pictures that resemble the characteristics of the university's walls.\
<img width="642" height="1058" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/55ccdda4-af75-4f9b-a03e-2221a546c8e8" />


## Narrowing it down

I used AI to give me a list of universities built with this kind of stone. I went through a bunch of them, and Bond University was next on the list. This is what I found.\
<img width="655" height="741" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9c41e7e2-da42-453c-8274-3382733097c1" />


On a closer look, everything matches the original photo: the library on the left, the business school on the right, the paving pattern on the ground, and the view in the background.\
<img width="1606" height="1123" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a776fef5-b5de-44f0-8218-7e5689f844de" />


## Result
The photo shows the central plaza of Bond University, located in Robina, on the Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia, with the John and Alison Kearney Main Library on the left and Bond Business School on the right.\
